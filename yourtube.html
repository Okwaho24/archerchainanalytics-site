Archer Chain Analytics — Product

YourTube™

AI-Powered YouTube Management Suite

Overview

Executive overview

YourTube™ is a production-ready, AI-powered YouTube management suite. It automates video uploads, schedules content, optimizes titles, descriptions and tags with AI, analyzes engagement metrics, manages playlists and tracks audience growth. It supports multiple channels with bulk operations, and adds comment sentiment analysis with AI-generated response suggestions.

Capabilities

Core capabilities

Automated uploads

Scheduled and bulk publishing, with AI-generated thumbnails.

AI content optimization

Titles, descriptions, tags and hashtags generated and optimized for search.

Analytics dashboard

Views, engagement, watch time, subscriber growth and revenue.

Comment sentiment analysis

AI sentiment classification with suggested responses.

Multi-channel management

Multiple YouTube channels administered from one dashboard.

Playlist curation

Automated playlist creation, ordering and visibility settings.
