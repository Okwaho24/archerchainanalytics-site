Archer Chain Analytics — Product

SalvageBotX™

Autonomous Digital Asset Recovery Engine

Overview

Executive overview

SalvageBotX™ is an autonomous recovery engine that identifies, traces and recovers lost, stolen or misallocated digital assets across blockchain networks. It draws on Alētheia Array™ for real-time chain monitoring, on AcerbE™ for forensic watermark verification, and on institutional-grade asset authentication.

Capabilities

Core capabilities

Asset tracing

Real-time blockchain monitoring of stolen or lost asset movement.

Watermark verification

Detection and verification of AcerbE™ forensic signatures as chain-of-custody proof.

Multi-chain recovery

Asset tracking across all 1,519 verified blockchain mainnets.

Forensic analysis

Institutional-grade investigation reports and evidence documentation.

Recovery coordination

Automated workflow for legal compliance and asset restitution.

Insurance integration

Claims documentation and coverage validation for digital-asset policies.

Applications

Target applications

Insurance claims

Verification of digital-asset theft in support of claims processing.

Enterprise security

Recovery of misallocated or stolen corporate digital assets.

Legal compliance

Forensic evidence for litigation and regulatory investigations.

Creator protection

Support for creators recovering stolen or pirated digital works.

Commercial

Commercial terms & deployment

Per-case pricing or an enterprise subscription. Forensic analysis, legal consultation and recovery coordination are included. Insurance partnerships are available.
