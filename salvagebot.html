SalvageBotX™

Autonomous Digital Asset Recovery Engine

●
Core Specifications

SalvageBotX™ is an autonomous recovery engine designed to identify, trace, and recover lost, stolen, or misallocated digital assets across blockchain networks. Leverages Alētheia Array™ for real-time chain monitoring, forensic watermark verification via AcerbE™, and institutional-grade asset authentication.
