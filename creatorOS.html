Archer Chain Analytics — Product

CreatorOS™

Creator Commerce & Monetization Platform

Overview

Executive overview

CreatorOS™ is an end-to-end commerce platform that enables independent creators to build, monetize and scale digital businesses. It integrates with RaPaX™ for product vending, AcerbE™ for IP protection and LexForge™ for compliance automation, and supports subscriptions, one-time sales, community access and fan engagement.

Capabilities

Core capabilities

Product management

Digital and physical products created, priced and managed without code.

Payment processing

Crypto and fiat accepted, with built-in RaPaX™ vending for instant digital delivery.

Fan community

Gated content, Discord integration, member-only access and tiered support.

IP protection

AcerbE™ watermarking applied to every digital product sold.

Compliance automation

LexForge™ generates legal documents for each jurisdiction automatically.

Analytics dashboard

Revenue tracking, customer insight and performance metrics.

Applications

Target applications

Digital creators

Musicians, artists and writers selling music, art, ebooks and exclusive content.

Online educators

Courses, workshops and community programs with membership access.

Software developers

Plugins, templates and open-source tools sold directly to users.

Content creators

Patreon-style support, exclusive video, livestreams and fan rewards.

Commercial

Commercial terms & deployment

SaaS or white-label licensing. Transaction fees of 3–5% or flat subscription pricing. Custom integrations and enterprise features are available.
