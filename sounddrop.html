SoundDrop™

Audio-First Short-Form Video Editor for Musicians

Overview

Executive overview

SoundDrop™ is an audio-first short-form video editor optimized for musicians and music creators. Users upload tracks, auto-generate visuals, sync them to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts, and publish to multiple platforms simultaneously. AI-powered audio analysis provides beat detection, vocal isolation and mood matching to visual effects.

Capabilities

Core capabilities

Audio-first editing

Upload a track and the video follows, with visuals auto-synced to beats and drops.

Beat detection

AI beat recognition for precise synchronization with visual transitions.

Visual-effects library

Thousands of music-aligned effects, filters and transitions.

Multi-platform publishing

Simultaneous export to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and more.

Stem separation

AI isolation of vocals, bass and drums for individual editing and remixing.

Creator monetization

Integrated with CreatorOS™ to monetize short-form content.

Applications

Target applications

Musicians

Short-form content from existing tracks, without video-production skills.

Music producers

Beats, loops and productions showcased across social platforms.

Streaming promotion

Short-form clips generated automatically to drive listeners to Spotify and Apple Music.

Music labels

Short-form content created in bulk for artist rosters across all platforms.

Commercial

Commercial terms & deployment

SaaS subscription with tiered feature access. White-label for music labels, DSP integrations and bulk creator licensing are available.
