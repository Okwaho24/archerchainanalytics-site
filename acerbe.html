Archer Chain Analytics — Product

AcerbE™

Forensic Watermarking & Tamper-Protection Engine

Overview

Executive overview

AcerbE™ is a production-ready forensic watermarking and tamper-protection engine for institutional intellectual-property protection. It embeds imperceptible, cryptographically anchored fingerprints in digital assets, binding every asset to a unique identifier tied to transaction metadata and blockchain verification. AcerbE™ is available standalone or embedded in RaPaX™ for automatic point-of-sale watermarking.

Capabilities

Core capabilities

Imperceptible fingerprinting

Cryptographically anchored identifiers embedded with no visible degradation of asset quality.

Tamper detection

Identifies unauthorized modification, duplication or distribution of protected assets.

Blockchain anchoring

Fingerprints are anchored to the Mahihkan™ proof wallet and distributed ledgers, creating a permanent audit trail.

RaPaX™ embedded mode

Automatic watermarking at the point of sale, integrated with vending workflows.

Multi-asset coverage

Protects software, digital media, documents and proprietary data formats.

Audit-grade records

Production-ready forensic audit logging and tamper-evident records.

Applications

Target applications

Software distribution

Unique identifiers embedded in software binaries support license verification and piracy detection.

Digital media protection

Artwork, music, video and documents are watermarked so unauthorized distribution can be traced.

Institutional compliance

Proof of authenticity and chain of custody for regulated financial and legal documents.

Creator IP protection

Creators can establish ownership and enforce their rights over digital works.

Commercial

Commercial terms & deployment

Available standalone or embedded in the RaPaX™ product suite. API-first architecture, custom asset-type integration and forensic audit services are available. White-label licensing is offered to enterprise partners.
