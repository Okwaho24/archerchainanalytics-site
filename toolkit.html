US Expansion Toolkit

A Canadian Founder's Playbook for Accessing US Funding

●
What It Is

A practical playbook for Canadian founders who want to build and fund a business in the United States. It covers two things: the new US banking regulations that have made the American system more accessible than it's ever been, and the exact sequence non-resident founders follow to form, bank, and build credit in the US.

Who it's for: Canadian founders — resident in Canada, no US Social Security Number — who want US business banking, US business credit, and access to deeper US capital markets.

What it is not: legal advice, financial advice, or a shortcut around underwriting. Banks still underwrite. Regulators still regulate. This toolkit tells you where the doors are and in what order to walk through them.

●
Inside the Toolkit
The GENIUS Act
The first comprehensive federal framework for payment stablecoins — regulated dollar rails your bank recognizes, including the Treasury interim final rule (FR Doc 2026-19966) on state certification up to $10B.
Fair Banking
The August 2025 executive order against politicized debanking — what it does and does not do for a founder walking into a bank.
The Sequence
Form the US entity, get an EIN without an SSN, open US business banking remotely, then build US business credit — in that order.
Form 5472
The filing a foreign-owned US LLC cannot skip — $25,000 per year in penalties, even with no tax owed.
The Canadian Side
A US LLC is flow-through in the US but treated as a corporation by the CRA — engage a Canadian CPA before you form, not after.
Verified Against the Source
Regulatory claims checked against the Federal Register, October 12, 2026 — not guru vibes.
