Archer Chain Analytics — Product

Archer Revenue Finder™

Multi-Marketplace Digital Asset Distribution Engine

Overview

Executive overview

Archer Revenue Finder™ is a multi-marketplace distribution engine that lets creators and teams publish and monetize digital assets across Gumroad, Etsy, Amazon KDP, Substack, Patreon and custom storefronts. It provides unified inventory management, pricing optimization and revenue consolidation, and supports both physical and digital products.

Capabilities

Core capabilities

Multi-channel distribution

Publish once to Gumroad, Etsy, Amazon KDP, Substack and Patreon.

Unified inventory

Stock levels across every marketplace managed from a single dashboard.

Pricing optimization

Price testing, competitor monitoring and margin optimization per marketplace.

Revenue consolidation

Sales data and payments aggregated into one unified ledger.

Analytics dashboard

Sales, margin and performance tracking across channels.

API integrations

Custom storefronts and fulfillment systems connected by webhook.

Applications

Target applications

Digital creators

Ebooks, courses and templates sold across multiple platforms at once.

Print-on-demand

Designs published to Etsy, Redbubble and Amazon Merch as recurring income streams.

SaaS companies

Side products, templates and plugins monetized across multiple marketplaces.

Creator networks

Products from multiple creators managed across all major distribution channels.

Commercial

Commercial terms & deployment

SaaS subscription per listing or a revenue-share model. Custom marketplace integrations and API access are included for enterprise partners.
