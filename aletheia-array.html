Archer Chain Analytics — Product

Alētheia Array™

1,519-Mainnet Blockchain Listener Network & Hall of Records

Overview

Executive overview

Alētheia Array™ is a multi-chain listener system covering 1,519 verified blockchain mainnets. It provides real-time transaction ingestion, cryptographic verification and append-only forensic ledger storage. A 19-document legal suite is delivered at point of sale in 19 languages, and the World Language Matrix for multilingual compliance is the platform’s principal competitive differentiator.
