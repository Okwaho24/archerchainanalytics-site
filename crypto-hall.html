Crypto Hall of Alexandria™

Mission

Executive overview

Crypto Hall of Alexandria™ is a sovereign, zero-trust cryptocurrency education library anchored to the Mahihkan™ proof wallet. It is the foundation of the Native Land Initiative, a humanitarian pillar dedicated to supporting vulnerable populations through cryptocurrency education, open-source infrastructure and financial empowerment. Transfer to Alētheia Foundation Inc., a federal not-for-profit corporation and CRA-registered charity, is planned.
