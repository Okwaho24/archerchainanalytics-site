Archer Chain Analytics — Product

RaPaX™

Sovereign Digital Products Vending Machine

Overview

Executive overview

RaPaX™ is a sovereign digital-products vending machine engineered for automated cryptocurrency-to-digital-asset delivery. Each transaction generates a unique payment address and triggers a 60-second cron poller for on-chain payment detection. AcerbE™ forensic watermarking is embedded automatically, and an expiring download link (a 96-character hex token) is delivered within seconds. An append-only SQLite audit log enforces strict immutability, with no DELETE or UPDATE operations.
